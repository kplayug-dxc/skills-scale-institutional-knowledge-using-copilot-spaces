# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Sponsor / Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors represent organizational and business interests, providing strategic context, funding decisions, and business priorities. They serve as the voice of the business and ensure projects align with broader organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters and business cases
- Define business success metrics and acceptance of outcomes
- Remove blockers at the business or organizational level
- Attend key decision gates and provide business input
- Communicate project status and outcomes to executive leadership
- Manage stakeholder expectations and alignment

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project delivery and organizational strategy
- Secure necessary resources and funding for approved projects
- Minimize business disruption and maximize return on investment

### Typical Communication
- Milestone updates and status summaries (high-level)
- Decision gate reviews and approvals
- Executive summaries and business impact reports
- Escalation of strategic business blockers

---

## QA/Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists own quality assurance, test strategy, and validation of acceptance criteria. They ensure that deliverables meet defined quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design comprehensive test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing across features
- Identify, document, and track defects and quality issues
- Validate quality gates and Definition of Done criteria
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test automation
- Perform smoke testing, regression testing, and end-to-end testing
- Report test results and quality metrics to the team

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Reduce production incidents and customer-facing defects
- Enable fast, reliable feedback loops during development
- Continuously improve test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and Definition of Done reviews
- Test result reports and quality dashboards
- Defect and issue tracking
- Collaboration on test strategy and automation frameworks

---

## Technical Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, solution architecture, and guidance on complex projects. They mentor developers, guide technology choices, and ensure solutions are scalable and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Design solution architecture aligned with requirements and constraints
- Review technical proposals and architecture decisions
- Guide technology choices and evaluate trade-offs
- Mentor and coach developers on technical best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and complexity
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and architectural principles
- Collaborate with Product and Project Managers on technical feasibility

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and performant technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and minimize rework
- Enable team growth through mentorship and knowledge sharing
- Balance innovation with stability and maintainability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Technical deep-dives on complex features or risks
- Code review guidance and mentorship
- Technology evaluation and research documentation

---

## Scrum Master / Process Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters coach teams on agile practices and facilitate ceremonies. They remove process blockers, foster psychological safety, and help teams optimize their productivity and ways of working.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, retrospectives, and reviews
- Coach team on agile principles and practices
- Identify and remove process blockers and impediments
- Track team velocity and process metrics
- Foster psychological safety and encourage continuous feedback
- Drive process improvements based on retrospective action items
- Support team culture and collaboration

### Goals
- Optimize team productivity and flow
- Improve process health and team engagement
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and psychological safety
- Enable self-organizing teams to make effective decisions

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospectives and process improvement discussions
- Velocity tracking and process metrics
- Facilitation of team problem-solving and conflict resolution

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure that security and compliance requirements are met throughout the project lifecycle. They manage security risks, conduct assessments, and maintain regulatory compliance.

### Responsibilities
- Review and define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Conduct security assessments and threat modeling
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Manage security items in the risk register
- Review architecture and design decisions for security implications
- Coordinate security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Support incident response and post-incident reviews
- Provide security training and awareness guidance to the team

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and data protection risks
- Maintain regulatory compliance and audit readiness
- Embed security practices throughout the development lifecycle
- Foster a security-conscious culture within the team

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and compliance reviews
- Risk registers and mitigation planning
- Security assessments and test results
- Incident response coordination and post-incident debriefs
